# Video poster frames

Drop the four customer-video poster frames here as:

    video-01.jpg   video-02.jpg   video-03.jpg   video-04.jpg

405 × 720 (9:16), under 150KB each. Pick a frame where the person's face is
visible and they are mid-sentence — a frozen smile reads as stock footage.

A tile whose poster fails to load hides itself, and while all four videos are
still `REPLACE_VIDEO_n` in index.html the whole reviews section stays hidden.
